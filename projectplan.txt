
Perfect! As your product manager, I’ve got you covered. Here’s a complete project plan for your barbershop booking app. We’ll break it down into key sections:

🧠 Product Overview
App Name (Tentative): BarberEase
Goal: Simplify haircut bookings, empower barbershop owners with tech tools, and enhance user experience using AI haircut suggestions.

🎯 Target Users
Customers: People looking to book haircuts, browse styles, and find trusted barbers.

Barbers: Professionals offering haircut services.

Barbershop Owners/Admins: Manage bookings, barbers, services.

Super Admins: Oversee platform, manage users, and ensure compliance.

🚀 Core Features
🔹 Customer Features
User registration & login (email, phone, or social login)

Browse barbershops by location/rating

View available barbers in a shop

Browse hairstyle inspirations

Book a haircut (select date, time, barber, style)

AI-powered haircut suggestions (LangChain + RAG)

Appointment status tracking (Pending, Confirmed, Finished, Canceled)

Wishlist/favorites (save barbershops or hairstyles)

Notifications (booking confirmations, reminders, promos)

View booking history

🔹 Barber Features
Accept/reject bookings

Set availability and working hours

View appointment schedule

Mark appointments as finished or canceled

Add photos of past work to their profile

🔹 Barbershop Owner/Admin Features
Add barbers to the shop

Set services and prices

Manage shop profile (name, logo, location)

View and manage appointments

Manage customer reviews and feedback

🔹 Platform Admin Features
Full access to all users, barbers, shops

Ban/unban users

Approve new barbershop registrations

View analytics (most booked barbers, active users)

Manage flagged content or reports

🧩 Tech Stack

Backend/Database: Supabase

AI Integration: LangChain + RAG with vector DB (e.g., Supabase Edge Functions)
